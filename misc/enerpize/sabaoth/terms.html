Purchase Order General Terms & Conditions

I. General Instruction

  • Purchase Order (PO) number must be quoted on all commercial documents (commercial & tax invoices, packing slip, delivery notices, waybills, package and shipping related documentations).
  • Invoices should be always emailed to accounts@sabaothbusiness.com.
  • Always liaise with gms@sabaothbusiness.com for expediting activities.
  • Always keep groupsabaoth@sabaothbusiness.com of any correspondence to SABAOTH BUSINESS.

II. Order Execution

  • Immediate Notice should be sent if unable to supply. More than 48 hours, the order is considered accepted if no turning down within that time.
  • Supplier represents and warrants that the Goods/Services shall be in accordance with the purchase order (quantity, price, technical requirements, Original Manufacturer Name, reference and part number), free from defects, of good quality material and labor.
  • Supplier is responsible for insuring goods as per applicable laws and to perform its obligations under the PO where insurance is necessary including without limitation, its indemnity obligations. So goes for taxes that taxing authority may assess against the Supplier or anyone acting on its behalf.
  • All non-confirming goods or services will be rejected at supplier’s expense.
  • Delays in delivery exceeding 15 days may lead to PO cancellation and all advance payment to be refunded if supplier’s negligence has been established. Force majeure and events beyond Supplier’s agility are not considered.
  • Supplier’s delivery notices and waybill attesting goods delivery (must include good descriptions, quantity ordered, actual quantity delivered and backorder notice) must be signed and stamped upon goods receipt and an original copy to remain with the purchaser. Service completion form duly dated, signed and stamped by the purchaser should be as well registered by the supplier upon service execution.
  • SABAOTH reserves the right to cancel all or any part of an order any time before the order is shipped or service execution is rendered by the Supplier without compensation to him.

III. Hazardous and Toxic Substances

  • Chemical substances, mixture or any material which may generate a release of a chemical substance or hazardous agent transfer is subject to Supplier providing all related current and complete Safety data sheet and label.
  • Packaging, labeling, and marking must be either from original manufacturer’s original packaging or a package of greater or equal strength and integrity. They must be sufficiently and efficiently constructed to ensure containment of the product throughout the entire transportation cycle and be properly closed to avoid leakage or loss according to manufacturer’s specifications. Performance packaging should be the rule. In addition packaging, labeling and marking must be respective of UN and the International Labor organization regulations in this area.
  • Hazardous substances transport, storing and handling must be obedient to International and locals rules.
  • Containers containing chemical product must be clearly identified as per the International Maritime Dangerous Goods Code. Offloading at port must be performed under such territory port regulation.

IV. Pricing, Payment, Invoicing and Governing law and jurisdiction

  • Acknowledged PO pricing must be under no circumstance unilaterally changed by the supplier. Prices remain fixed and firm once a purchase order is issued within supplier quote validity period or upon written agreement with supplier prior to the order issuance. Prices are not subject to any escalation or variation.
  • Invoice must be respective of quantity delivered and purchase order pricing. It must be submitted along with the proof of delivery duly signed, stamped and dated from SABAOTH or its identified third party receiving the goods. Invoice addressed to SABAOTH must contain the following information:
    • Supplier or vendor full name and address as per contract or purchase order
    • Incoterm 2020 as provided as per Order
    • An invoice title number and date
    • The purchase order or contract number
    • Description of the goods, services or any cost provided by SABAOTH
    • Invoice amounts as per purchase order currency
    • Full bank details
  • Payment in general shall occur upon 30 days from receipt of an invoice or when agreed as identified in the purchase order.
  • All aspects of the purchase order or contract shall be governed by the laws of Burkina Faso.
  • Delays in payment or dispute of any matter shall be amicably settled and if not as under the rules of the International Chamber of commerce by one or more arbitrators appointed according to the said rules.
  • The seat of the arbitration shall be agreed by both parties. If no agreement, Paris in France shall be the place and either French or English shall be the language of arbitration.
